About BloodConnect

BloodConnect is an online blood donor registration portal designed to make it easier to register potential blood donors and search for donors based on blood group and location.

The application works directly in the web browser and stores donor information locally on the device using browser storage technology.

Important Note

This is an academic project. Donor eligibility and medical suitability must always be confirmed by qualified healthcare professionals or authorized blood banks.
