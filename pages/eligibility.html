Blood Donation Eligibility

General information for people considering blood donation.

Before Donating

Donor eligibility can vary depending on the blood bank, country, health condition, medications, travel history, and other factors.

👤

Age

Donors must meet the minimum and maximum age requirements specified by the authorized blood donation organization.

❤️

Health

Donors should generally be in suitable health at the time of donation and pass the required screening.

🥗

Preparation

Follow the instructions provided by the blood bank, including guidance about food, fluids, rest and identification.

Important

This page provides general project information and does not determine whether an individual is medically eligible to donate blood. Final eligibility must be determined by a qualified healthcare professional or authorized blood bank.
